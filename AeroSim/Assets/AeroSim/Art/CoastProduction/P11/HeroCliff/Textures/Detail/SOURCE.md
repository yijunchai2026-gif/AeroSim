# Hero Cliff detail scan source

The four `T_HeroCliff_P11_Detail*` textures are the 2K JPG maps from Poly Haven’s **Cliff Side** material: diffuse, OpenGL normal, roughness, and displacement. The source identifies it as a CC0 stratified rock scan with a physical width of 1.8 m::cite[1].

Source: https://polyhaven.com/a/cliff_side
